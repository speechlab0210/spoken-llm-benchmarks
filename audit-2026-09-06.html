核對方法與限制

  • Read the author-affiliation blocks for all benchmark papers; use fresh PDFs or contribution sections to adjudicate stale or incomplete HTML.
  • Read each non-cascade model's own report, model card or official release; distinguish academic collaborators from explicitly later employers.
  • Re-read all current arXiv records. Check publication candidates against publisher-deposited DOI metadata, ACL Anthology, ISCA Archive, ICLR proceedings, CVF, PMLR and official conference programs.
  • Use exact paper identity, including title changes and authors; a same-team publication is not interchangeable with the catalogued paper.
