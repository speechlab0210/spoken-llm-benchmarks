RESEARCH PROPOSAL / NOT AN EXPERIMENTAL RESULT研究提案 / 尚非實驗結果

Can a validated audit help us choose a better speech system?經驗證的稽核，能幫我們選出更好的語音系統嗎？

01 / Declare the claim01 / 先定義主張

Specify the capability, population and use. A changed speaker may be harmless for content recognition but consequential for identity verification.固定能力、族群與用途。同樣換一個語者，對內容辨識與身分驗證的意義可能完全不同。

02 / Pair defects with valid controls02 / 缺陷搭配有效對照

Compare wrong answer keys, text leakage or missing events with matched harmless edits. Include natural defects, not only synthetic mutations.錯誤答案、文字洩漏、漏掉關鍵事件，要搭配程度相似的無害編輯；也納入自然缺陷，不能只測人造變異。

03 / Hold out the mechanism03 / 保留未見失效機制

Split sources, speakers, model families and defect mechanisms before mutation. Compare auditors with the same visible information and budget.注入缺陷前先切分來源、語者、模型家族與失效機制；各稽核方法使用相同資訊與預算。

04 / Measure detection and false alarms04 / 同時量測抓錯與誤報

Report sensitivity, false positives, source-level uncertainty and cost. Many variants from one source are not many independent benchmarks.回報敏感度、誤報率、來源層級不確定性與成本；同一來源的大量變體，不是大量獨立 benchmark。

05 / Repair, then test the decision05 / 修復後再驗決策

Compare audit-guided repair with original and equal-budget generic repair. Evaluate selected systems on an independent target task.比較原版、依稽核修復與同預算一般修復；最後在獨立目標任務上，檢查選出的系統是否更好。

06 / Bound the novelty claim06 / 限定新穎性主張

Controlled degradation and mutation testing already exist. The proposed contribution is speech-specific audit validation tied to decision quality.受控退化與 mutation testing 都已有先例；候選貢獻是把語音特有的稽核驗證，連到實際決策品質。

The “best” target-system score is also an estimate. Account for selection and target-test uncertainty. No claim of first-of-its-kind novelty or demonstrated improvement is made. Closest starting points: 目標任務中「最佳模型」的分數也是估計值，需考慮選擇與測試的不確定性。本頁不宣稱首創，也不宣稱已證實改善。最接近的先例：conversational benchmark auditing · GateTruth · predictive validity.

04 / SCOPE & PROVENANCE

What this collection contains這份蒐集包含什麼

Two AI-assisted surveys completed on 22 September 2026 are merged here: a broad arXiv collection and a focused benchmark-quality survey with speech applications. The broad collection contributes 240 retained papers; the focused survey adds 10 after deduplication; two historical speech references from ISCA Archive complete the 252-record catalogue.合併 2026 年 9 月 22 日完成的兩份 AI 輔助 survey：一份廣泛的 arXiv 蒐集，以及一份聚焦 benchmark 品質與語音應用的調查。前者保留 240 篇，後者去重後增加 10 篇，再補上 ISCA Archive 的兩篇語音歷史文獻，共 252 筆。

  • Title/ID matching establishes bibliographic identity, not correctness. The 24 reading notes are an AI synthesis with explicit boundaries.篇名與 ID 核對只確認書目身分，不證明研究正確；24 篇導讀是附有解讀界線的 AI 綜合整理。
  • The catalogue includes methods, surveys and contextual papers—not 252 benchmarks or audit tools.清單包含方法、survey 與背景文獻，並不是 252 個 benchmark 或稽核工具。
  • This is a dated convenience sample. Its year distribution does not estimate field growth, and missing papers do not establish novelty.這是有日期的便利樣本；年份分布不能估計領域成長，沒找到的文獻也不能當作新穎性證明。
